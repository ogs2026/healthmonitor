**Full description:**

Hubble Health is a private daily health companion inspired by life on long-duration space missions.

DAILY CHECK-IN
Record blood pressure, resting heart rate, blood glucose, body temperature, sleep and mood in one quick check-in. See which readings are still due today.

YOUR PERSONAL RANGES
Set your own reference ranges. Each reading is labeled as within or outside your usual range, so changes are easy to spot.

TRENDS AND HISTORY
A dashboard with your latest blood pressure, a recent trend and the history of every indicator.

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDES
Clear guides for taking each measurement consistently, plus background on how altered gravity, isolation and confinement can affect the heart, bones, brain and skin.

SPACE HEALTH LIBRARY
Plain-language summaries of public NASA Human Research Program and Canadian Space Agency resources: the five hazards of human spaceflight, the main health risks, ongoing research, radiation monitoring and why we do science in space. Each topic links to the readings you track and to the original agency pages.

MORE TOOLS
• Dated skin and symptom photos to compare changes over time
• Nutrition log
• Private reflection journal
• Daily routine checklist

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device, and you can delete it all with one tap.

Hubble Health is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or the Canadian Space Agency. It helps you organize your own observations. It does not diagnose any condition and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
