Hubble Health ("the app") is published by **[YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app.

## The short version

- Everything you enter stays **on your device**.
- We have **no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**.
- We **cannot see, collect, sell or share** your health information.
- You can delete everything at any time from **Profile → Delete all my data**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Information you enter

The app lets you record:

- Health readings: blood pressure, resting heart rate, blood glucose, body temperature, sleep duration and mood, with optional notes.
- Your name, mission start date and personal reference ranges.
- Photos of skin or symptom changes that you take or choose.
- Nutrition notes, private reflections and daily routine check-offs.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If your device's operating system backs up app data (for example iCloud or Google device backup), that backup is handled by Apple or Google under your account settings and their privacy policies, not by us.

## Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you tap **Take photo** or **Choose photo**. The photos you select are copied into the app's private storage on your device. The app does not upload them anywhere.

## Health information and medical use

The app helps you organize your own observations. It does not provide a diagnosis or medical advice and is not a medical device. Always follow the guidance of a qualified health professional.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, because the app does not collect information at all.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. The Space health library contains links to public NASA and Canadian Space Agency web pages. If you tap one, it opens in your browser, and those sites have their own privacy policies. The app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) may collect information about your download under their own privacy policies.

## Deleting your data

Open **Profile → Delete all my data**, or uninstall the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts sending data off your device, we will update this policy and ask for your consent in the app first.
